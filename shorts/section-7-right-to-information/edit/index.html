Everyone!
I'm Rogie Domin,
and today,
let's talk about
Bill of Rights
Section 7,
or the Right to
Information.
So basically,
this means that
every Filipino citizen
has the right
to know
what our government
is doing,
especially
when it involves
public money.
For example,
let's say na
atong gobyerno
nag-allocate ug
10 million pesos
para sa
construction sa road.
Of course,
we have the
right to ask
kung asa padulong
ang kwarta,
giunsa ni siya paggasto,
and whether
the project
is actually
being completed.
But remember,
this right has
limitations
because some
government information
is confidential
and protected
by law.
So, why
is this important?
Well,
it promotes
transparency,
accountability,
and helps prevent
corruption.
At the end of the day,
the government serves
the people,
and we have
the right to know
how it operates.
