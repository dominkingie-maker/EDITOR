# Style guide — measured reference

Measured from three reference Shorts (~33 s each, 1080x1920 class, 30 fps) of the
"Simply Animated" format. Use these numbers when making creative choices the
templates don't already make for you.

## Structure
- Length 30–35 s. 4–6 independent scenes; average scene 4.7–6.5 s.
- Each scene = one visual metaphor + one caption idea. No narrator, no face, no intro.
- Frame 0 already shows the first visual with motion — the hook is the image.
- Frequent device: **contrast** — split screen (top vs bottom), "A vs B" labels,
  one lit figure among dim ones.
- Endings: either a payoff in the accent colour ("SUCCESS" ring turns gold) or a
  dark punchline. The last scene fades to black, which makes the loop seamless.

## Transitions
- Zero hard cuts. Scene change = fade out ~0.4 s → 1–4 black frames → fade in ~0.5 s.
- Rare exception: a sideways push/slide *within* one idea (before → after).
- Camera mostly locked. Motion comes from the figures; occasional slow pan or zoom-out
  (one lit window → whole building) as a reveal.

## Captions
- One short line at the top third, content centred below it.
- Revealed word by word, starting just after the visual appears; a second line
  (the payoff) is added a beat later beneath the first.
- Small, quiet typography: clean sans (or spaced monospace caps for variation).
  Never big bold "TikTok" captions.
- Faint channel watermark at bottom centre.

## Visuals
- Pure black background. White/grey pictogram stick figures with thick limbs,
  separate round head, darker back limbs (depth).
- Soft bloom/glow on light sources: doors, beams, windows, the hero figure.
- Greyscale palette + at most one accent colour (teal, gold) reserved for the key
  moment.

## Audio
- Music only, no voiceover. The reference videos reused one track: ~123 BPM, F
  key, building steadily and roughly doubling in loudness around 16–20 s (the drop).
- Scene changes follow musical phrases loosely, not individual beats; list-style
  sequences (e.g. LEARN → PLAN → DISCIPLINE → SUCCESS) change exactly every bar
  (~2 s at 123 BPM).
- Reusing the same track every episode builds recognition.
