# Visual ideas bank

About 150 visual metaphors grouped by theme. The point of this file is **variety**: start
from the message you want to land, look at its theme (and a neighbouring one), and pick
the idea that is most surprising while still readable in one second. `[T: name]` means a
ready-made template exists; everything else is built as a custom scene (see
`custom-scenes.md`) — most ideas below need only lines, circles, a figure, a prop or two
from `scripts/props.py` and one motion. Run `scripts/suggest.py` to get a shuffled shortlist
that skips recently used templates (see SKILL.md).

Each entry: **visual** — what moves · *motion type*.

## Consistency & habits
- **Water drop carving stone** `[T: water_drop]` — a drop falls again and again; a groove deepens until it breaks through · *repetition*
- **Snowball** `[T: snowball]` — a tiny ball rolls downhill, growing until it fills the frame · *growth along a path*
- **Braided rope** — single threads snap when pulled; braided together, the figure can't break them · *physics test*
- **Tally marks → word** — tally marks pile up on a wall; zoom out and they form the word "YES" · *zoom-out reveal*
- **Sharpening the axe** — one figure sharpens (sparks) while another hacks with a dull axe; one clean swing fells the tree · *split, payoff*
- **Footprints in snow** — snow falls and fills other trails; one trail keeps extending · *erasure vs persistence*
- Domino chain, small to huge `[T: domino]` · Streak calendar `[T: habit_grid]` · Staircase `[T: stairs]`

## Starting & procrastination

- **Couch quicksand** — figure on a couch sinks slowly, scrolling, until only the phone glow is left · *slow sink*
- **Red light that never turns** — figure waits at a traffic light stuck on red ("Perfect timing"); another walks past and keeps going · *contrast*
- **Snooze sun** — alarm rings, hand slams it, sun races across the sky, repeat; day is gone · *time-lapse*

## Shot & motion vocabulary
Plan each episode to mix at least 3 motion types and 2 shot types from these lists.

**Shot types:** wide side view · close-up then pull back (`camera()`) · top-down
(maze, map) · split screen (top/bottom or left/right) · cutaway (underground, inside a
body or building) · silhouette against a light · text-led (list, counter, single word) ·
single object macro (one candle, one domino).

**Motion types:** walk/run journey · chain reaction · growth (roots, plants, snowball) ·
transformation (cocoon, coal→diamond) · zoom-out reveal · wipe/clip reveal · physics
(falling, swinging, tipping, bouncing) · swarm/particles · counter or ticking list ·
convergence (rays to a point) · subtraction (things dropping away) · loop/repeat.
